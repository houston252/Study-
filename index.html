How to use this app

Start with the Scripture focus, spend a moment in prayer, and choose one concrete act of obedience. Check off practices as you do them. In the evening, write a reflection.

This is a simple devotional companion, not a substitute for reading the Bible, prayer, Christian community, or pastoral guidance.

Read in your Bible

Begin with Matthew 6:25–34, Matthew 11:28–30, John 15:1–17, and Luke 10:25–37. The short quotations in this app are from the public-domain King James Version.

Privacy and backup

Your entries stay in this browser on this device. They aren't sent to a server. Use Export my entries to make a backup. Clearing website data or using another browser can erase or separate your entries.
